## Why

P07's commissioned compatibility probes show that direct reuse of the existing
per-resource provider cannot retire even its six-kind control graph within one
original ordinary Decision. Namespace I/O is also outside that counter. Resolve
the whole-registration accounting policy before dependent retirement code.

## What Changes

- Propose a source-selected infrastructure accounting boundary, distinct from
  existing proof/readback phases, whose durable original scope covers registration,
  state, provider, fence, receipt, revocation, preservation and final audit calls.
- Require a complete first-pilot call/byte/time inventory, explicit numeric limits
  and exact paired source acceptance before selecting any new phase or limit.
  This draft selects no replacement number and permits no cap increase/reset.
- Realize already accepted separate cleanup authority and current read-only late
  observation through additive owning adapter paths; preserve setup-window refusal,
  original cleanup expiry, one-send attempts and old receipt/profile field sets.
- Preserve the measured partial outcome and every predecessor policy. A new
  infrastructure allowance cannot be borrowed by runtime proof or admitted by a
  request, environment flag, plugin, callback, retry, restart or report.
- Keep the sole implementation handoff at existing openXwallet feature017 T013P07.
  Scope is the first codeXfactory machine pilot; P08/P09 retain their gates.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `infrastructure-retirement-accounting`: eligibility and source-selection rules
  for complete, metered, bounded infrastructure retirement without renewing old
  proof/setup authority or hiding backend I/O.

### Modified Capabilities

None under canonical openspec/specs. The additive candidate composes accepted
P3 R07/R09/R10/R11/R12. It does not rewrite the frozen parent packet,
predecessor budget amendments, neutral contracts or existing runtime limits.

## Impact

Factory owns the source policy. openXwallet owns the later isolated implementation
through feature017. The [eligibility record](supporting-docs/eligibility.md) contains
six passing diagnostics, the actual original-budget refusal and independent
24-store teardown audit. These are neither full P07 qualification nor a measured
full first-pilot graph. The draft is not ready for ratification of a new cap:
[open selections](design.md#open-questions) must be resolved first.

This local packet follows the accepted P07 eligibility fallback. Publication,
ledger completion with a real PR identity, hosted CI, local review and exact
paired source acceptance remain separate. No task/operational acceptance moves.
Setup/dispatch unauthorized; feedback deferred; blocked writes unreplayed.
