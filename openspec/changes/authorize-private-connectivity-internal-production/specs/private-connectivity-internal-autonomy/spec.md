## Purpose

Allow a genuinely authorized delegate to complete private connectivity for
bounded internal production use while preserving actual evidence, operating
authority, rollout order and explicit human ownership.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Delegation has a finite genuine human root
Autonomous owner decisions and operations SHALL require the exact ratified
human grant, selected policy, real delegate, accountable owner, target and
financial/support scope. The grant SHALL be finite and revocable. A receipt
or delegate SHALL NOT nominate a different root, expand its scope or claim
that the human reviewed an observation that did not yet exist.

#### Scenario: Actual selected internal authorization
- **WHEN** the genuine human grant is active and every operation predicate matches its selected scope
- **THEN** only the bounded internal operation or fresh delegated decision SHALL be eligible

#### Scenario: Caller substitutes or expands the grant
- **WHEN** the root, actor, validity, customer, target, support or financial selection differs or is revoked
- **THEN** delegated acceptance and new effects SHALL be refused

### Requirement: Delegated acceptance retains actual independent evidence
A delegated decision SHALL identify its actual actor/time, root authority,
immutable candidate/scope/output and independent review. Required CI and
operating conclusions SHALL be authenticated from actual producer/provider
evidence. Structural validation, a copied verdict, missing review, quota
failure or source/unit evidence SHALL NOT establish observed runtime success.

#### Scenario: Fresh source output is independently qualified
- **WHEN** exact-head checks, actual command output and independent review satisfy the current source policy
- **THEN** the delegate SHALL record a distinct current delegated admission without changing original human provenance

#### Scenario: Observation or review is missing
- **WHEN** an expected control, monitoring interval, actual producer or independent review is absent or failed
- **THEN** the consuming acceptance SHALL remain refused

### Requirement: Internal autonomy preserves gate and authority order
The delegate SHALL retain Q0 commissioning, released candidate/harness closure,
fresh governed target authority, bounded Q1 staging, actual four-path selected
matrix Q2 qualification, separate internal Q3 canary and observed Q4 acceptance.
Every effect SHALL remain within current channel/grant/consent and resource/data
limits. Missing capacity or credentials SHALL NOT be replaced by a lab stub,
unsafe state reuse, broader permission or a claimed future observation.

#### Scenario: Staging is accepted
- **WHEN** actual Q2 evidence and all original prerequisites pass
- **THEN** only a separate exact internal canary decision SHALL become eligible

#### Scenario: A target is not admitted
- **WHEN** durable state, existing capacity, current authority or compatible supported inputs cannot be established
- **THEN** that target operation SHALL stop without widening the envelope

### Requirement: Source and release autonomy keeps review constraints
Within the exact feature purpose the delegate SHALL obtain independent exact-file
amendment review and refresh the complete definition before using new source
scope. Ready/merge/release SHALL require exact-head checks/review and dependency
order, normal immutable publication and actual readback. Admin override SHALL
address only missing independent GitHub approval, never failed checks, conflicts
or unresolved findings. Required checks and original source-proof privacy
SHALL NOT be weakened.

#### Scenario: Source is ready to land
- **WHEN** actual required checks/review and all source dependencies pass under the human grant
- **THEN** the exact scoped PR can be readied and merged with its post-merge verification

#### Scenario: A required check fails
- **WHEN** a required check, review finding or integration dependency is unresolved
- **THEN** landing SHALL remain blocked while scoped repairs and fresh validation proceed

### Requirement: Internal completion does not activate external expansion
The initial supported internal rollout SHALL use the explicitly selected
Windows/Linux profile and SHALL leave deferred macOS combinations unqualified.
Internal completion SHALL require all three offerings actually usable with
observed controls and operator handoff. External Q5 offers, customer enrollment,
prices and expansion SHALL remain gated and their unexecuted tasks SHALL NOT
be marked complete.

#### Scenario: Internal production succeeds
- **WHEN** all three selected internal delivery tracks pass their actual Q4 acceptance and handoff
- **THEN** the run SHALL report internal completion and retain the explicit deferred support/external-Q5 work

#### Scenario: An external customer is proposed
- **WHEN** a customer, offer or wave is outside the named internal scope
- **THEN** a separate human commercial/scope decision SHALL be required before activation

### Requirement: Failures preserve containment and current revocations
A failed or out-of-scope result SHALL halt affected admission/expansion and
preserve authorized neighbors, current revocations and immutable history.
A delegated resume SHALL require actual independent remediation review,
affected/neighbor reruns and fresh authority within the unchanged policy.
No financial, identity, scope or observation exception SHALL be manufactured.

#### Scenario: A scoped repair is validated
- **WHEN** actual reviewed remediation and reruns pass within the remaining current authority
- **THEN** a fresh exact delegated resume decision SHALL be eligible

#### Scenario: Repair needs broader authority
- **WHEN** repair exceeds the approved customer, resources, finances or security boundary
- **THEN** the delegate SHALL stop that work and require a human ruling
