## Proposed bounded staging envelope

Subject to exact owner approval, use existing QA in West US, two synthetic
customers, no customer/regulated data, at most two networks and three devices
per customer. Use isolated namespaces, keys, endpoints, policy and durable
instance state. Keep independent network entitlement and application permission.

The first standalone customer A is created without a factory subscription.
Customer B has no pre-existing network before the actual managed-new/domain-new
control. Then test existing-network attachments and independent cancellation.
A failed attempt preserves its original subject set and cleanup identity.

Proposed aggregate requests are at most 2000m CPU/4 GiB memory and limits at most
4000m/8 GiB on existing admitted regular capacity. No pool growth, preemption,
system/Spot fallback or new public IP/load balancer is allowed. Current
schedulability, requests, volume slots, quota, approved namespace admission and
neighbor health are Q1 prerequisites.

The existing accepted financial envelope is USD 10 storage-only for a maximum
14-day run: up to eight 4-GiB disks, 32 billed GB snapshots and 10 billed GB
objects, with retained capacity costed for one month. Actual SKU billing,
network/logging allowances and retained cost must fit before approval. Financial
consent does not provision resources or authorize a retention lock.
